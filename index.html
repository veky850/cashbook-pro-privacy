ARTICLE I

Regulatory Framework & Architectural Preamble

This Enterprise Privacy Policy ("Charter") forms a legally binding covenant between CashBook Pro ("Application", "Platform", or "Service") and the commercial licensee, sole proprietor, or organizational entity operating the software ("Enterprise User").

Unlike traditional software-as-a-service (SaaS) accounting suites that ingest proprietary ledger entries onto remote central servers, CashBook Pro executes its foundational data operations within an isolated, sandboxed local database environment. Remote cloud synchronizations, document transmissions, and telemetric verifications occur strictly upon user-prompted affirmative instruction.

ARTICLE II

Local Cryptographic Storage & Database Isolation

All accounting records created within the Platform reside in an isolated SQLite relational database powered by the Android Room v21 engine. The boundary constraints governing local storage are delineated as follows:

  • Sandbox Isolation: The local database is encapsulated within the private internal storage of the Android operating system, preventing unauthorized third-party applications from reading, modifying, or inspecting ledger entities.
  • Cryptographic Backups (AES-256): Standalone backup archives (.cbk) generated for cold storage are sealed utilizing PBKDF2 key derivation and military-grade Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) 256-bit Galois/Counter Mode (GCM) encryption.
  • Tamper-Proof Audit Logging: Financial ledger modifications, transaction reversals, and administrative deletions generate immutable audit log records documenting user authorization tiers, timestamps, and ledger balance differentials.
ARTICLE III

Smart Assistant Operations & Network Security

CashBook Pro incorporates an optional smart assistant and document optical recognition engine to streamline automated bookkeeping, voice vouchers, and supplier invoice intake:

  • Ephemeral Processing: Prompts transmitted for invoice parsing or natural language ledger booking are processed purely ephemerally over encrypted TLS 1.3 channels.
  • Zero Persistent Model Training: Financial records, party titles, balances, and voucher amounts sent for processing are not utilized to train foundation models or retained in model provider databases.
  • Direct Client-Side Keys: Users retain the affirmative option to input their personal enterprise API credential within the application settings, bypassing any central intermediate proxies.
ARTICLE IV

Google Play Policy Compliance & User Permissions

The Platform adheres rigorously to the Google Play Developer Program Policies, User Data Policies, and Limited Use requirements:

  • No Broad Storage Permissions: The application does not request dangerous broad external storage access. Media imports (invoices, receipts) strictly use the zero-permission Android Photo Picker.
  • Microphone Access: Microphone permissions are activated solely when the user affirmatively initiates voice voucher input and are immediately released upon completion.
  • Biometric Authentication: Local biometric verification leverages Android BiometricPrompt hardware abstraction enclaves; raw biometric hashes never leave device hardware.
ARTICLE V

Data Sovereignty, Export & Total Deletion

You maintain complete sovereignty over your commercial data at all times:

  • Full Ledger Portability: Export complete accounting statements, transaction journals, and customer aging ledgers at any time in standardized PDF, Excel (XLSX), or JSON formats.
  • Immediate Deletion: Clearing app data or utilizing the in-app "Reset Database" command executes a permanent cryptographic overwrite of all local tables and cached vouchers.

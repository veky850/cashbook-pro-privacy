04 Permissions and choices

Android permission requests observed in the application include camera, microphone, notifications, biometrics, storage/media access on relevant Android versions, network connectivity and advertising ID. Certain privileged features may require separate system settings, such as notification-listener access.

You can review or change permissions in Android Settings. Denying an optional permission can limit the specific feature that requires it. The app does not need unrelated permissions to provide basic ledger functions.

05 Storage, security and transfers

CashBook Pro uses device storage for financial records and supports connected cloud features. We use available security controls and secure transmission mechanisms where implemented. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their account, device and exported records.

We aim to use appropriate access controls and secure transport when handling information. No application, device, or network service can guarantee absolute security. Users should secure their device and avoid sharing exported documents with unauthorized recipients.

Cloud and AI processing may occur on servers outside your country, subject to relevant service-provider terms, jurisdiction and configurations. Specific international-transfer mechanisms require further legal and technical validation.

06 Retention, correction and deletion

Data may be kept on the device while the app remains installed, in account-connected services while the account is active, and in backups or exports created by the user. Third-party SDKs may apply their own retention rules.

You may update relevant records within the application, export your data using available export features, or initiate an account deletion request using the section below. Deleted records may remain in separately retained user-created exports or external recipients' copies. Any legal or fraud-prevention retention exception must be identified and limited to what is necessary.

Deletion requests: Some records are stored in different services and may require separate cleanup steps. We verify account ownership before processing requests and address any incomplete deletion operations. Data subject to applicable legal retention obligations may be retained only as necessary.

Everyday use and transparency

09 Why information is used

CashBook Pro processes information to let you create and manage financial books, record cash received or paid, organize customer and supplier accounts, produce reports and invoices, and restore information where supported. Account details are used for sign-in and account-related functions. Optional connected features may require information to be sent outside the device.

Core bookkeeping

When you enter a transaction, the app may store its title, date, amount, type, category, notes, relevant party and ledger reference. These records may contribute to balances, daily summaries, reports, charts and exports. The accuracy of records also depends on information you enter and any corrections you make.

Optional assistance and automation

AI-assisted finance queries, receipt processing, speech recognition, notifications, forecasts, document sharing, and backups can use additional information. These are not equivalent to ordinary local record entry; consult the feature description and applicable permissions before using them.

Service reliability and abuse prevention

Account and diagnostic information may be handled to support sign-in, maintain service operation, investigate failures, prevent unauthorized access and respond to support inquiries. The exact logs and retention periods depend on the implemented service and provider configuration.

Business data handling

10 Ledgers, parties, invoices and reports

Financial records can include cash inflow/outflow, balances, transaction descriptions, party contact details, invoice line items, receipt images, tax-related fields, stock or inventory information, staff-related entries and notes. Your use of these features may involve information about customers, suppliers, employees or family members.

You are responsible for entering such information lawfully and informing relevant people where required. Avoid storing passwords, PINs, payment-card security codes or unnecessarily sensitive personal information in free-text notes.

PDF, spreadsheets and sharing

When you export financial data, the resulting file may contain the selected transaction history and identifying details. Files sent through email, messaging applications, printers or third-party storage are handled by the recipients and services you choose. Removing a record from CashBook Pro does not automatically recall an exported file from its recipients.

Financial and AI outputs

Forecasts, anomaly indicators, generated summaries and AI explanations may be inaccurate or incomplete. They are provided as informational aids and should be checked against source transactions before tax, legal, lending or professional accounting use. AI prompts may be processed by an external provider when the connected assistant is used.

14 Account & Data Deletion

CashBook Pro account holders may request deletion of their account and associated data through the app or through the contact method below. This page also explains how to make a request without installing or accessing the app.

Request deletion by email

Send a request from the email address associated with your CashBook Pro account, if possible. Tell us that you want your account and associated data deleted. Never send passwords, PINs, OTPs or recovery codes.

Email account deletion request

Option 1 — Delete from the application

  1. Open CashBook Pro and sign in.
  2. Open Settings and find the account deletion action.
  3. Follow the confirmation and any required reauthentication steps.
  4. Review the result displayed by the app. If the deletion does not complete, use the email method above.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

  1. Send an email to waqaskasuri850@gmail.com with the subject CashBook Pro Account Deletion Request.
  2. Provide the registered email address and state that you request deletion of the account and related information.
  3. Complete reasonable ownership verification if requested. Never disclose passwords or security codes.
  4. We will review the request and communicate its outcome or any unresolved steps.

Information covered

Depending on your use of CashBook Pro, deletion may involve authentication details, user-profile information, cloud accounting records, uploaded receipts and documents, and developer-controlled backups associated with your account. We will identify applicable legal or security-related retention exceptions where required.

What may remain outside our control?

Exports you saved separately, files shared with other people, independently managed third-party copies, and certain data subject to lawful retention requirements may not be removable through a CashBook Pro account deletion request. Google Drive backups may require access to the connected Google account or further action.

Before deleting

Export any financial records you need to keep for your own tax, legal or business records. Account deletion can be irreversible once completed. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete cloud account data.

Important: A request does not guarantee instant deletion of all records. If an operation fails or additional verification is required, the request must be followed up rather than marked completed. Contact us if you need an update.

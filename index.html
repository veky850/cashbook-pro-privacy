Privacy Policy

Welcome to CashBook Pro ("Apka Digital Bahi Khata"). Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Sovereignty & Offline-First Policy

CashBook Pro operates primarily as an offline-first application. Your financial records, including ledger entries, customer details, and transaction history, are stored locally on your device in a secured sandbox. We do not transmit this data to our servers unless you explicitly enable Cloud Synchronization features.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you sign in via Google, we collect your email address and profile name to manage your subscription and license.
  • Cloud Backups: If you enable Google Drive sync, the app uploads your encrypted database to your own private Google Drive storage. We do not have access to these files.
  • Device Information: We collect an app-scoped unique identifier to manage multi-device licensing. This identifier is not linked to your physical hardware ID (IMEI/Serial).

3. Permissions Usage

  • Camera/Storage: Used solely to attach receipt photos to your transactions via the Android Photo Picker.
  • Microphone: Used only when you trigger the "Voice Khata" feature for voice-to-text input. Audio is processed locally.
  • Contacts: Optional permission to help you quickly add parties to your ledger.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (AES-256) for local backups. Cloud communication is secured via TLS/SSL. All data stored in Firebase is protected by strict security rules and is accessible only through your authenticated account.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the absolute right to access, export, or delete your data at any time. A "Delete Account" feature is available in the Settings menu, which permanently removes all your records from our Firebase servers, Google Drive backups, and your local device.

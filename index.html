ARTICLE I

Regulatory Framework & Architectural Preamble

This Enterprise Privacy Policy ("Charter") forms a legally binding covenant between CashBook Pro ("Application", "Platform", or "Service") and the commercial licensee, sole proprietor, or organizational entity operating the software ("Enterprise User").

Unlike traditional software-as-a-service (SaaS) accounting suites that ingest proprietary ledger entries onto remote central servers, CashBook Pro executes its foundational data operations within an isolated, sandboxed local database environment. Remote cloud synchronizations, document transmissions, and telemetric verifications occur strictly upon user-prompted affirmative instruction.

ARTICLE II

Local Cryptographic Storage & Database Isolation

All accounting records created within the Platform reside in an isolated SQLite relational database powered by the Android Room v21 engine. The boundary constraints governing local storage are delineated as follows:

  • Sandbox Isolation: The local database is encapsulated within the private internal storage of the Android operating system, preventing unauthorized third-party applications from reading, modifying, or inspecting ledger entities.
  • Cryptographic Backups (AES-256): Standalone backup archives (.cbk) generated for cold storage are sealed utilizing PBKDF2 key derivation and military-grade Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) 256-bit Galois/Counter Mode (GCM) encryption.
  • Tamper-Proof Audit Logging: Financial ledger modifications, transaction reversals, and administrative deletions generate immutable audit log records documenting user authorization tiers, timestamps, and ledger balance differentials.

ARTICLE IV

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Administrative Privileges

To safeguard commercial accounting integrity across multi-employee retail and enterprise operations, CashBook Pro enforces strict Role-Based Access Control:

  • Master Administrative Owner: Holds sovereign credentialing, biometric unlock authority, unlimited financial dashboard access, audit inspection rights, and exclusive control over database eradication protocols.
  • Branch Manager: Authorized to record ledger entries, review party accounts, and compile PDF statements. Strictly restricted from executing global database wipes or modifying system security architecture.
  • Counter Cashier: Restricted strictly to entering current-day sales and counter receipts. Cashiers are cryptographically barred from viewing consolidated corporate profits, modifying historical records, or inspecting global analytics.
ARTICLE V

Automated Bank SMS Sync & Notification Telemetry

Where voluntarily authorized by the Enterprise User, the Platform operates a localized Bank SMS & Notification Auto-Sync Listener:

Zero Interception Guarantee: The listener filters and inspects incoming messages strictly originating from verified commercial banking alphanumeric sender headers. Non-financial personal SMS communications, private chat messages, and two-factor authentication (2FA) verification OTP codes are programmatically dropped and immediately ignored.

Parsed transaction values (amount, timestamp, debit/credit orientation) are recorded directly into your local database. At no point are raw text payloads or customer financial communications uploaded, mirrored, or analyzed on external servers.

ARTICLE VI

Cognitive Intelligence, Neural Speech & Optical OCR Processing

CashBook Pro integrates intelligent accounting features designed with strict data privacy boundaries:

  • AI Financial Chatbot: The conversational financial analyst answers queries regarding ledger balances, profit trajectories, and outstanding credit. The conversation runs strictly within local session context; business metrics are not retained or utilized to train external machine learning foundation models.
  • Voice Khata (Speech-to-Text): Employs native on-device Android SpeechRecognizer services to transcribe spoken ledger commands into structured database entries. Audio waveforms are transiently processed in volatile memory and are purged instantly post-transcription.
  • Smart Document OCR Scanner: Optical character recognition executed on invoice photographs analyzes receipt totals and dates directly on local hardware without uploading unencrypted business receipts to third-party endpoints.
ARTICLE VII

Global Multi-Currency Valuation & Live Forex Telemetry

The Platform incorporates an automated multi-currency conversion matrix covering over 160 international sovereign fiat currencies:

Forex rate queries utilize transport-layer encrypted (TLS 1.3) REST endpoints against authorized global exchange feeds. Telemetric request headers carry exclusively standard ISO-4217 currency identifiers (e.g., PKR, USD, AED, EUR). No account identifier, ledger value, balance sheet sum, or hardware signature is appended to currency telemetry calls.

ARTICLE VIII

Cloud Repositories & Private Google Drive Integration

Cloud synchronization is voluntary, user-initiated, and governed by zero-knowledge access barriers:

  • Google Drive AppData Sandbox: Cloud archives reside within the user's isolated Google Drive Application Data Folder (appDataFolder). This enclave is accessible solely by the authenticated user's Google token. CashBook Pro software engineers and platform operators possess zero technical capability or legal authorization to view or decrypt Drive backup files.
  • Firebase Infrastructure Security: Where realtime multi-device sync is licensed, data transmission operates exclusively under TLS 1.3 cryptographic enforcement backed by strict granular server-side rules restricting database access exclusively to authenticated account owners.

ARTICLE X

Statutory User Rights & Absolute Data Eradication

In strict conformance with international data privacy frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and global commercial data governance codes:

  • Right to Universal Portability: The Enterprise User retains the unrestricted prerogative to export complete accounting records into non-proprietary formats (CSV, Excel, PDF) at any juncture.
